Chord Game

The Problem

Notes are easy, chords are not

Most beginner music games train note-by-note recognition. Chords — three or more notes played together — are where harmony actually happens, and they're rarely practiced directly, so learners can name a note but freeze on a triad.

Features

What it does

01
Multi-Key Chords
Keys A S D F G H J K map to the white piano keys C through C, pressed simultaneously.
02
Chord Recognition
Built around holding combinations down together, training finger coordination over sequential reading.
03
Progression Practice
Structured around chord progressions so players build harmonic intuition, not just reflexes.

How it works

Players hold multiple keys at once to form a chord, mirroring how hands actually move on a real keyboard. The simultaneous-press requirement forces recognition of the chord shape as a whole, rather than hunting for one note at a time.
