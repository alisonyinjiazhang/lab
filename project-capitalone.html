The Problem

Training buried in menus

Corporate L&D tools have a low bar and usually clear it by being merely functional. Courses, HR tasks, and team resources tend to live in separate places, so getting through required training means bouncing between tools before you even reach the content.

The Approach

One dashboard, not five tabs

Brought it into a single hub: a course catalog with mixed video and reading lessons, a Team Resources page, and Quick HR & Admin Reminders surfaced right on the dashboard instead of buried in a separate portal. A built-in review flow lets employees leave feedback right after finishing a course, and an in-app chat covers questions that don't fit neatly into a course page.
