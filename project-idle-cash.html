The Problem

Mass customers do not change how they invest

J.P. Morgan is a leading firm for wealthy investors. Everyday banking customers are less confident, and their money often sits idle. Wealth Plan needed to coach those customers — starting with Idle Cash, the first Next Best Action insight I designed from 0–1, then templatized for related features.

The shipped design

Calculation, education, and guidance

The idle cash tool explains what idle cash is, shows the breakdown, and how to use it — the screens that shipped in Wealth Plan.

The Story

Education when they are not ready to move money

The tool explains what idle cash is, breaks down the amount, and suggests how to use it while keeping the customer’s lifestyle. For people who still hesitate, a short story layer adds bite-sized education — cards, scrollable photos, and branded video — instead of another form. That story format came from testing outside the design system, then was brought back in as a reusable insight template.

The Approach

Use Chase data, not a generic nudge

Competitive work showed Chase’s edge: existing customer data and advisor expertise. The logic tells someone how much they can invest over the long term without changing how they live today. The trigger is overspending anxiety after a large purchase; the product answers with a transfer amount and a destination account tied to their goals. After Idle Cash shipped, the same template covered market volatility, job-change / 401(k) rollover, and a wall of insights on the dashboard.
