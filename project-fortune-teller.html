Fortune Teller

A tarot reading experience that pairs your zodiac sign with a three-card spread for past, present, and future.

The Concept

Personal before mystical

Most online tarot generators feel generic — a static deck with no personalization. This one starts from something personal, your zodiac sign, before revealing anything, so the reading feels tailored rather than random.

Features

What it does

01
Zodiac-First Flow
Choosing a sign unlocks topic selection, personalizing the reading from step one.
02
Three-Card Spread
Past, present, and future cards narrated as a single continuous journey.
03
Regenerate
Users can pull a new spread anytime without restarting the whole flow.

How it works

The reading unlocks in steps — sign, then topic, then cards — so each choice feels like it's shaping the outcome. Cards like The Moon, The Star, and The Sun are framed with celestial theming and Roman numerals to keep the tone consistent throughout.
