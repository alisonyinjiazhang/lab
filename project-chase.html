Chase Wealth Plan

Wealth Plan is a J.P. Morgan Chase tool for building and tracking a financial plan with an advisor — retirement, education, and healthcare goals in one plan instead of three separate ones.

The Problem

Investors underestimate retirement spending

That was the starting problem statement, and it showed up in the product as three specific issues. Retirement, education, and healthcare goals were kept technically separate even when a client's real plan combined them. Clients could not edit their own goal details without contacting an advisor. And inputs like Social Security amount were collected during onboarding, disconnected from the goal that actually needed them.

The Approach

What I did

As the product designer on Wealth Plan, I led the redesign of how goals came together, letting related goals combine into a single plan instead of staying siloed. I rebuilt goal setup so clients could manage more of their own plan, without waiting on an advisor for every change.
