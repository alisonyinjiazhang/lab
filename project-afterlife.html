The Problem

No plan for what's left behind

68% of people have no digital estate plan, and an estimated $58B in digital assets goes unclaimed every year — mostly because there's no clear, trusted way to hand off access to accounts, crypto, and personal files when someone dies.

Features

What it does

01
Asset Vault
Accounts, property, crypto, and insurance stored with masked values until unlocked.
02
Encrypted Letters
Personal messages that unlock on specific triggers: death, birthdays, milestones.
03
Guardian System
Two trusted contacts each hold a key fragment; both must verify before the vault opens.

How it works

A user assigns assets and letters to beneficiaries, then selects two guardians to hold key fragments. When a trigger event is confirmed, both guardians cooperatively unlock the vault, notifying beneficiaries and revealing the masked assets, messages, and instructions inside.
