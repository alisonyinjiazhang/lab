Cost of Living

A side-by-side calculator for comparing take-home pay, rent affordability, and career growth across up to four cities at once.

Role
Design & Development
Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Type
Web Tool
Focus
Finance · Cities
View
Live Site

The Problem

Same salary, different life

People weighing job offers or relocation decisions rarely compare cities on equal footing. A $120k salary doesn't stretch the same way once state taxes and local rent are factored in — but most offer comparisons stop at the headline number.

Features

What it does

01
Tax Comparison
Combines federal and state tax brackets to show real take-home pay per city.
02
Rent Affordability
Flags whether rent crosses the standard 30%-of-income threshold for each city.
03
5-Year Growth
Projects how salary and savings might compound over five years per location.

How it works

Users add up to four city cards, entering a salary for each. The tool runs every card through the same tax, rent, and growth model in parallel, so the comparison stays apples-to-apples instead of relying on rough mental math.
