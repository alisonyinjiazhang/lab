Introduction

Alison Zhang

For the past eight years I've worked in product design at Chase, Coach, and BitGo. By combining product strategy, design aesthetics, and front-end execution, I produce apps with complete product lifecycle from concept to deployment now with deep domain knowledge in Finance and E-commerce.

Growing up in the 90s shaped a visual style that balances tech brutalism with minimalist design. I am drawn to the clean lines of early digital iPod interfaces, bringing that same tactile precision into contemporary web environments through stark layouts and intentional micro-interactions.

Outside of work, I make music.
